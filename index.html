RESEARCH APPROACH

I start with the business, then work toward the investment thesis.

01

Understand the business

What does the company sell, who pays it, and how does it generate revenue?

02

Find the growth engine

What trends or changes could meaningfully affect the company's growth over the next 5–10 years?

03

Test the strengths

What gives the company an advantage, and how durable are those advantages?

04

Identify the risks

What could slow growth, reduce returns, or challenge the company's position?

05

Form a thesis

I weigh the company's opportunities against its risks before reaching my investment conclusion.

ABOUT THE PORTFOLIO

Built as an independent student research project.

This portfolio documents my process of researching public companies and building investment theses from business fundamentals, growth trends, financial information, strengths, and risks.

The goal is not to predict the market. It is to understand how a business works, identify what could drive its future, and explain the reasoning behind an investment view.
