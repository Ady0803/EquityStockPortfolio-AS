AAPL

Apple

Equity Research Report · June 7, 2026

Business Overview

  • Hardware: IPhones, Macs, iPads, Watches, Wearables/Audio accessories, home accessories
  • Subscriptions/Media Services: Apple TV+, Apple Music, Apple Arcade, Apple Fitness+, Apple News+
  • Software Tools & Utilities: App Store, xCode, Swift Playground, Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, Facetime, iMessage, Maps, Wallet, Find My, Siri, Safari, Freeform, iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, visionOS, tvOS
  • Finance: Apple Pay, Apply Card, Apple Cash, Apple account balance
  • Support: AppleCare

How the Company Makes Money

The 2 biggest ways Apple makes its money is by selling hardware devices and monetizing those same users who bought their devices through recurring services. The other ways it earns money is through app store purchase commissions in which Apple takes 15-30% of the digital purchases. Along with this the company also earns money from first-party subscriptions, and financial as well as ad fees. Lastly it also earns billions of dollars annually through hidden traffic deals, for example google which pays it billions to remain the default search engine on Safari.

My Investment Opinion

My investment rating on Apple is a 7. I would invest in Apple as it’s a company that has room to grow in certain markets like India where other technology companies are at a large. As a result there is a lot of room for growth and as a result more money to be made. According to Apple's financials their revenue grows every year backing this.

AI Summary

Apple is a global technology company that sells devices like iPhone and Mac and provides digital services. Its growth opportunities include expansion into emerging markets like India, growth in services, and AI integration across products. Its main risks include heavy dependence on iPhone sales, strong competition, and regulatory pressure on its App Store business.

Strengths & Risks

  • Strength: Very strong cash flow
  • Strength: Extremely loyal customer base
  • Strength: High room for improvement in locations such as some countries in Asia, company will only create more profits not less for a long time
  • Risk: Large debt, but manageable to due to large cash flow
  • Risk: May be hard to grow in other markets not due to bad product but lack technology and government factors
  • Risk: Strong reliance of Iphone sales, lack of advancement to its Iphones which it seems it is getting harder to do can largely impact its sales

Growth Trends

A trend that could help Apple grow is its expansion into Global Markets where its footprint isn’t as large. A place like this includes India where its market shares aren’t nearly as large as other places in the world. Not to mention the market in India is only increasing which leaves a lot of room for growth. Along with this as AI expands and continuously evolves a lot of Apple's features such as Siri will also evolve becoming more desirable. Also the evolution of AI leaves a lot of room for Apple to incorporate it into a lot of their other products, an example of how they could do this is shown through the meta glasses which originate from another company.

About the Company: Competitors

Apple’s biggest competitors include Samsung, Google, Microsoft, and Amazon. Along with this there exists other hardware competitors depending on the region. For example, in the asian markets there exists Huawei, Xiaomi, and Lenovo.

Growth Story

Investors are interested in this company because of its fiercely loyal customer base, its highly profitable service division, and its reliable history of extreme money making. On top of this Apple generates roughly $100 billion in free cash flow, the company also regularly rewards investors by returning enormous amounts in regular dividends. On top of this there is still even more global market expansion in areas where Apple's footprint isn’t that large like India.

Final Verdict

Overall, Apple appears to be a financially strong company and an attractive long-term investment due to its growth potential. It has a large, loyal, and consistent customer base and has demonstrated strong long-term revenue growth. It also has room to grow in emerging markets such as India, which could increase future sales and cash flow. This should help Apple manage its debt while continuing to invest in new technologies and services that support its long-term growth.
